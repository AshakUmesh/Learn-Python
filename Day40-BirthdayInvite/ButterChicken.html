Finger Lickin’ Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Delicious restaurant-style butter chicken made right at home with simple ingredients. This recipe is exactly how they make butter chicken in restaurants — and I’ve had people tell me it’s actually better! Trust me when I say it’s finger lickin’ good!

Ingredients

Chicken Marinade:

  • 1 lb. boneless chicken breast, cut into 1 ½ inch cubes
  • 2 tablespoons tandoori masala
  • 1 teaspoon EACH: ginger paste and garlic paste
  • ½ cup yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon oil

Butter Chicken Sauce:

  • 2 tablespoons ghee
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 ½ teaspoons EACH: ginger paste and garlic paste
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons coriander powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons cumin powder
  • ½ cup heavy whipping cream
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • ¼ teaspoon dried fenugreek leaves

Instructions

Chicken:

  1. In a medium bowl, combine tandoori masala, ginger, garlic, and yogurt. Whisk until smooth and adjust seasonings to taste. Add chicken and marinate for at least 20 minutes (ideally 12–24 hours in the fridge).

Butter Chicken Sauce:

  1. Heat ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add onions and sauté for 5–7 minutes until translucent. Add ginger and garlic paste, stir for 30 seconds. Add crushed tomatoes, chili powder, coriander, and cumin. Cook for 5 minutes. If bubbling rapidly, add ¼ cup water.
  2. Remove from heat and blend until smooth, adding up to ¼ cup water if needed. Blend in two batches if necessary. Hold blender lid with a towel to prevent spills.
  3. MAKE AHEAD: Sauce can be cooled and refrigerated for 24–48 hours or frozen for up to 3 months. Bring to room temp before continuing.

Assembly:

  1. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in the pot over medium heat. Add marinated chicken (discard excess marinade). Cook for 5–6 minutes until browned. Add sauce and heat through. When bubbling, stir in cream and garam masala. Simmer, then add fenugreek. Serve with basmati rice or naan.

Notes

  • Chicken Thigh Option: Use boneless, skinless thighs for more flavor.
  • Ghee Substitute: Use 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp oil if ghee is unavailable.
  • Chili Powder: Optional. Tandoori masala already adds heat. Use Indian chili or cayenne; reduce to ¼–½ tsp for milder taste.
  • Tandoori Masala: Available in powder or paste (e.g., Patak’s). Use 2–4 tbsp of paste to preference.
